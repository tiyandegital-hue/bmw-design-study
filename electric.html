Charging, honestly

Ten minutes is a coffee, not a compromise.

Both cars charge at up to 200 kW DC. On the iX xDrive50, BMW's own press material puts it plainly: as much as 150 km of range added in ten minutes. Home charging at 11 kW AC fills either car overnight. Range figures are WLTP — the laboratory standard every maker is measured by, printed next to the number wherever we use it.
