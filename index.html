The cockpit

Everything turns toward the driver.

For fifty years the BMW cockpit has angled its controls at one person. Displays, switches, the road ahead — arranged around the seat doing the work.

Electric, at full voltage.

The iX adds up to 150 km of range in ten minutes of DC charging (BMW press material). The i4 goes up to 590 km on a charge. The future of the brand is not quieter — it is faster, with better numbers.
