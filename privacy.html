Privacy policy

Study template, written the way a real one should read: plainly.

What this demo collects

Nothing. The test-drive form validates in your browser and discards its input. This static site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party trackers — every font, script and image is served from this site's own files.

What a real version would collect

Only what a booking needs: your name, one contact channel, the model and the date. It would be used to arrange the drive and for nothing else — no sale of data, no marketing list without separate consent — and deleted after the booking concludes.

Your rights

In a live service you could ask what is held about you, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time, through the same channel you booked with.
